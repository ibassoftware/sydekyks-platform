Sydekyks uses the **Sydekyks Sustainable Use License 1.0** for new original
contributions covered by [LICENSE.sydekyks.txt](LICENSE.sydekyks.txt). It is a
source-available license with restrictions on competing hosting and software
resale. All available features remain free for permitted self-hosted use.

## MIT baseline

The last platform commit before this transition is
[`f2e657c9c18447efc7167440b9481503f23dfc8a`](https://github.com/ibassoftware/sydekyks-platform/tree/f2e657c9c18447efc7167440b9481503f23dfc8a).
Its previously granted MIT permissions remain in effect. The already published
`ibassoftware/sydekyks:1.0.6` image is not relicensed by this documentation change.

This transition adds licensing and documentation; it introduces no new
functional application code. It therefore does not make the existing MIT
platform implementation subject to new hosting or resale restrictions. Users
can continue using, modifying, distributing, selling, and hosting the MIT
baseline under its original terms, including retained notices.

## Contributions after the transition

Beginning with the commit that introduces `LICENSE.sydekyks.txt`, new original
project contributions are offered under that license unless an explicit
file-level license or written contribution agreement states otherwise. This
applies only to contributions whose copyright holders can grant those rights.
An existing MIT portion remains MIT even when a new, separately licensed
addition is made to the same file. Merely moving, renaming, reformatting,
rebuilding, or copying MIT code does not make it restricted code.

Upstream Activepieces code remains under the retained
[MIT notice](LICENSE.activepieces.txt). Third-party dependencies, integrations,
fonts, documentation, and other assets retain their applicable licenses.
Restricted Activepieces enterprise directories remain excluded from this
distribution. This license does not grant rights to those upstream modules.

Future releases must retain all applicable notices and identify covered
contributions and their source revision. The license transition does not
introduce activation, feature gates, seat limits, or licensing network calls.

## Partners and services

Installation, customization, training, and support on a customer-owned and
controlled installation are permitted services. Authorized partners may also
provide hosting, managed services, and resale within the scope of their written
partner or reseller agreements. The agreements, rather than this public
license, contain commissions and commercial service terms.

This document explains scope; the actual licenses and valid written agreements
govern. The Sydekyks license is custom license text, not an OSI-approved license
or a claim of independent legal review.
